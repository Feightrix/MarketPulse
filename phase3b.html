What Phase 3B proved

Simply becoming more selective did reduce drawdowns and trading frequency, but it did not create a stable edge. The failure across both 2024 and 2025—and 0/9 nearby exit settings remaining profitable—means this exact price-action family is not robust enough.

What it did correctly

At sufficiently high assumed execution costs, the cost-buffer rule refuses to trade rather than knowingly enter a setup whose expected gross move cannot justify the friction. No trade is preferable to a structurally negative trade.

Historical simulations do not guarantee future returns. Phase 3B remains research-only. No real-money trading has been enabled, and this failed gate does not authorize paper-order automation.
