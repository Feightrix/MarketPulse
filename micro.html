What the data is telling us

1 · Tiny targets are too fragileWhen the gross edge is only a few basis points, spread/slippage can consume it.
2 · More trades ≠ more consistencyThe first micro setup generated losses in both development and validation despite frequent small exits.
3 · We need a larger edge per tradeThe next iteration should remain intraday, but trade less often and demand stronger setup quality before entry.

Proposed Phase 3B direction

Keep the micro / small-win philosophy, but move from pure scalping to selective intraday micro trades: stronger regime filter, fewer entries, volatility-adjusted targets/stops, and a minimum expected edge that must clear a friction buffer before a trade is allowed.
