What PASS Means

PASS means: the strategy remained profitable in training, validation, and an untouched holdout; held the holdout drawdown under 25%; achieved a holdout Sharpe above 0.45; and had at least a 60% historical bootstrap probability of a positive one-year result.

PASS does not mean: guaranteed profit, a guaranteed monthly income, or readiness to trade real money. The next validation layer must stress-test execution, alternate data periods, harsher slippage, parameter instability, and failure scenarios.
